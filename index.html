Calescent

Geothermal heat rights, San Francisco

We rank land next to known geothermal fields better. We don't find new systems. We find the parcels beside existing ones where the heat is most likely to keep going, and we lock up the rights before anyone drills.

What we do

  • Read every public temperature log we can get: state well files, old exploration reports, water wells, oil and gas holes. Most of it sits in scanned PDFs nobody has digitized.
  • Turn those readings into a temperature-at-depth estimate for every few square kilometers of the western US, with an honest error bar on each one.
  • Take cheap options on the rights where the estimate is hot and the land is still open, mostly from private owners who have never been asked.
  • Drill shallow temperature holes to prove the best ones, then lease or sell the proven rights to the companies that build power plants.

Why now

Data centers need firm, clean power, and geothermal is one of the few sources that runs around the clock. More developers are out looking for sites, and the land next to known fields is where they look first. Whoever holds the rights when a developer shows up gets paid.

Where we are

We have built the data pipeline and the temperature model, tested it by freezing the data at past dates and checking against wells drilled later, and are now starting landowner outreach and our first temperature holes in Nevada, Utah, California and Oregon.
